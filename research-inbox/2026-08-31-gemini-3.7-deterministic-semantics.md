[Original chat content]
Semantic search fundamentally maps textual intent to textual relevance based on meaning rather than exact token overlap; the problem reduces to vocabulary mismatch, compositional meaning, polysemy. The ~0.34 ceiling without dense encoders is a known IR boundary (lexical plateaus ~0.28-0.35 on semantic datasets).
Paradigms: (1) distributed continuous (neural embeddings), (2) topological/symbolic (graphs/ontologies, typed relations, PPR), (3) algebraic/distributional (deterministic matrix decompositions).
Ceiling causes: synonymy/paraphrase barrier; polysemy/context collapse; compositional negation/modifiers; asymmetric query-document distributions.
Deterministic approaches: LSA/truncated SVD over TF-IDF/PPMI (closed-form synonym clustering via higher-order co-occurrence); VSA/HDC (role-filler binding, bundling, D>=10k); graph propagation (Personalized PageRank over ConceptNet-style typed ontologies); ESA (explicit concept-space projection over a curated KB).
Strategies: (1) dual-index routing — BM25 intersected with 2-hop typed-ontology walk; (2) PPMI query rewriting — offline co-occurrence expansion above strict threshold; (3) syntactic dependency chunking — SVO predicate-argument tuple matching.
